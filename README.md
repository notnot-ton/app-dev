# Favorite Series: High Potential

**Genre:** Crime / Drama / Comedy  
**Rating:** 7.8/10  

## Overview
*High Potential* follows Morgan, a single mother with an exceptional mind and a High Intellectual Potential (HIP), whose unconventional knack for solving crimes leads her to partner with a seasoned detective at the LAPD.

### Main Characters
1. **Morgan Gillory** - A brilliant single mom with an IQ of 160 turned police consultant.
2. **Detective Karadec** - The thorough, by-the-book lead investigator working alongside Morgan.
3. **Lieutenant Selena** - The supportive captain who brings Morgan onto the team.

### Why It's Great
- **Fast-paced mystery:** Every episode features unique, complex cases.
- **Witty dialogue:** The contrast between Morgan's chaotic genius and Karadec's strict methods makes for dynamic chemistry.
- **Relatable struggles:** Balances high-stakes investigation with real-life family dynamics.

> *"My brain just works differently—I see patterns where others see chaos."*
